    How we cook

    We fry to order. If you can hear the fryer, your plate is being made.

    The fish

    We buy from day boats and fillet each morning in the kitchen. What is not on the board by close is gone, so the last plates of the night might be plaice instead of cod.

    The chips

    Maris Piper potatoes, cut by hand, blanched, rested and fried a second time. Fluffy in the middle and crisp enough to hear.

    The fryer

    Rapeseed oil, filtered every day and changed twice a week. Ask for beef dripping and we will fry yours in that instead.
